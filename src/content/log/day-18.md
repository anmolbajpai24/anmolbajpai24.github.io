π0.7 came out in April. It's Physical Intelligence's latest robot model, and
apparently it operated a kitchen appliance it was never trained on. I spent
today reading up on how.

It can operate an air fryer. You give π0.7 the task, then correct it mid-run
in plain language, or show it a picture of the subgoal, and it adjusts. Its
training data for the task traced back to **two episodes** of someone
closing an air fryer, plus open robot footage. With coaching, it loaded a
sweet potato into a machine it had never used.

Two episodes. My network needed 800 demos to lift one cube, and it had to be
handed the cube's coordinates first.

This was a reading day. I didn't run the model.
